## Overview

This generative design project visualizes the impact of light pollution on bird migration patterns. Using p5.js for real-time generation and After Effects for post-production, the project makes an invisible environmental issue visible and compelling.

## Challenge

Light pollution disrupts bird navigation during migration, causing disorientation and increased mortality. However, this issue is difficult to communicate because its effects are invisible to casual observers and happen at night when humans aren't watching.

## Approach

### Research & Data
- Studied research on bird migration and light pollution effects
- Analyzed flight pattern disruption data
- Translated scientific findings into visual parameters

### Generative System Design
- Built particle system representing bird flocks in p5.js
- Implemented attraction forces simulating light pollution effects
- Created parameters for natural vs. disrupted flight patterns

### Visual Development
- Designed aesthetic that balances scientific accuracy and visual impact
- Used After Effects for final composition and animation
- Created contrast between natural patterns and disrupted behavior

## Results

- Successfully visualized complex environmental phenomenon
- Demonstrated power of generative design for science communication
- Combined technical coding skills with design storytelling

## Technologies

- p5.js (Creative Coding)
- After Effects (Motion Graphics)
- Particle Systems
- Data Visualization

## Key Learnings

This project taught me how code can be a powerful medium for storytelling. By translating scientific data into generative parameters, complex issues become tangible and emotionally resonant. The intersection of coding, design, and environmental awareness opened new perspectives on creative technology's role in communication.
